SCDOT Bridge Inspection Guidance Document (BIGD) 2026 — Inspector & Reviewer Essentials

4 PDH · Course no. SC-BI-BIGD · Author: Michael Craig, PE (placeholder)

A PE/NICET-oriented walk-through of the May/2026 SCDOT BIGD: purpose and definitions, roles (BIPM, DBIS, BITL), inspection intervals and reduce/extend rules, Critical Findings and repair flags, and Chapter 8 QC/QA — cited from BIGD only.

Learning objectives

  • State the BIGD purpose and disclaimer limits (uniformity under NBIS; not a substitute for engineering judgment)
  • Apply BIGD definitions of Bridge, Culvert, NSTM, Critical Finding, and Undersized Bridge
  • Map SCDOT roles — BIPM, LRE, BMQE, SBIS, DBIS, BITL, ABI — to inspection, CF tracking, and load-rating coordination
  • Select typical intervals and reduce/extend triggers from Tables 3–13/26 (routine 24M; UW 60M; NSTM 24M; tolerance 1–2 months)
  • Classify repair Priority A/B/C (Table 30), distinguish Priority A vs Critical Finding, and apply CF discovery / FHWA 24-hour notify / address (30 days) rules
  • List BIGD load-rating request triggers (§§4.11, 7.3.1) as inspection follow-up (cross-link to LRGD)
  • Distinguish QC vs QA under Ch 8 and name annual field-review / independent-inspection expectations

How the course works

  1. Short lesson screens with a side menu and progress tracking
  2. Knowledge checks every few screens, with instant feedback
  3. Seat time recorded for your PDH record (~200 min active → 4 PDH)
  4. Final quiz unlocks once every screen is complete (15 questions, 70% to pass, unlimited retakes)
