SCDOT Load Rating Guidance Document (LRGD) & AASHTO MBE Practice

4 PDH · Course no. SC-LR-LRGD · Author: Michael Craig, PE (placeholder)

SCDOT-specific companion to Load Rating Fundamentals (BR-101): LRGD purpose and scope vs MBE, PE/EOR ≠ QC rules, when to re-rate, LRFR-first method selection (§6.9.3), live-load suites, LRSF deliverables, and posting / Critical Finding posting handoffs — cited from LRGD 2019 and BIGD where they interact.

Learning objectives

  • State the purpose and scope of the SCDOT LRGD relative to the AASHTO MBE and when to ask the SBME for coordination
  • Decide when a new rating or re-rating is required for new, existing, and rehabilitated bridges, including inspection-driven triggers
  • Select LRFR, LFR, or ASR per SCDOT policy; distinguish Inventory vs Operating; identify SCDOT legal/SHV/EV/permit load suites at guidance level
  • Describe SCDOT QC/QA roles, preferred-software policy, and the Load Rating Summary Form (LRSF) deliverable package
  • Apply posting and posting-avoidance procedure at guidance level, including Signing/Posting Form flow and the 3-ton minimum / closure threshold

How the course works

  1. Short lesson screens with a side menu and progress tracking
  2. Knowledge checks every few screens, with instant feedback
  3. Seat time recorded for your PDH record (~200 min active → 4 PDH)
  4. Final quiz unlocks once every screen is complete (15 questions, 70% to pass, unlimited retakes)

Outline

  1. Lesson 1 — LRGD purpose, scope, PE quals, TN rule Sample (~0.75 hr)
  2. Lesson 2 — Process, data, method selection (§6.9.3 quiz-critical) (~1.25 hr)
  3. Lesson 3 — QC/QA, LRSF, deliverables (~1.0 hr)
  4. Lesson 4 — Posting & inspection handoffs (~1.0 hr)
  5. Final quiz Sample (5 of 15) — 70% to pass

Prerequisite. Working familiarity with AASHTO MBE load-rating concepts. SpanPDH Load Rating Fundamentals (BR-101) recommended.
