Bridge Inspection

For team leaders, program managers and NICET Bridge Safety Inspection technicians. Practical skills from a working inspection PE, with an ethics course built for inspectors.

Honest credit note: These courses count toward PE renewal (in states that don't require provider approval) and can be claimed for NICET CPD. They are not FHWA/NBIS bridge inspection refresher training — under 23 CFR 650.309, only NHI or FHWA-approved state courses count toward the 18 refresher hours.
